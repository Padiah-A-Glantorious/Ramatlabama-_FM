System Walkthrough & User Guide

Listener Website & Station Portal

The platform has two parts: the Listener site (what the public sees and hears) and the Station Portal (where staff log in to run the station, at yourdomain.co.za/portal). Access levels: God Admin (top authority), Elevated Access (admins), and Presenter. Managers see all tabs; plain presenters do not see the Shows tab.

PART 1 — THE STAFF PORTAL

Staff open yourdomain.co.za/portal and log in. Every portal screen shows, across the top: Welcome, [Name] with their title, a gold access badge, a Log out button, and the tab row Chat · News · Shows · Members · Settings.

Chat tab

The live listener message feed (read-only for staff).

  • Shows Listener Chat — live requests, shout-outs and dedications as listeners post them.
  • Before anyone posts it reads "Listener requests appear here."
  • Read-only by design — staff respond verbally on air, not by typing.
Buttons: Chat / News / Shows / Members / Settings switch screens; Log out ends the session.

News tab

Edit the "top stories" that scroll on the listener site. Everyone can edit news.

Each story row has four fields: Tag (e.g. LIVE), Date, Headline, and Detail.

Buttons:
  • + Add story — adds a new empty story row.
  • ✕ (red) — removes that story.
  • Save news — saves all stories; goes live on the ticker instantly.
Nothing saves until you press Save news. A "✓ Saved — live on site" message confirms it.

Shows tab (managers only)

Manage the on-air schedule — shows and timeslots.

Three separate schedules via the top switch: Weekday (Mon–Fri), Saturday, Sunday. Each show row has Start time, End time, Show name, and Presenter.

Buttons:
  • Weekday / Saturday / Sunday — switch which day you're editing.
  • + Add [day] show — adds a show row to that day.
  • ✕ (red) — removes a show.
  • Save shows — saves all three schedules.
The listener site picks Saturday, Sunday, or weekday by the real day, and tags whichever show is on air as "ON NOW".

Members tab

See the team and manage access. Presenters view only; managers can enrol, elevate, revoke, delete, and edit titles.

Enrol form (managers): Email (their login), Full name, Title, then + Enrol. New presenters sign in with their email and the shared password.

Each member card shows a name, a gold access badge, and an editable title box.

Buttons (managers, where allowed):
  • ↑ Elevate — grants a presenter Elevated (admin) access.
  • ↓ Revoke to presenter — removes elevated access; keeps the account as a base presenter.
  • 🗑 Delete — permanently removes the account.
  • Title box — type and click away to save a new title.
The top God Admin account can never be deleted and shows no buttons to others. Elevated users manage everyone except the God Admin; the God Admin manages everyone.

Settings tab

Update your own display name and password.

Buttons:
  • Save name — saves your new display name.
  • Change password — needs your current password, then the new one.
Every account can change its own password here. Change the default on first login.
PART 2 — THE LISTENER SITE

What the public sees at yourdomain.co.za. Audio keeps playing while visitors scroll or chat.

Home — Now Broadcasting & On Air

The top of the listener page.

  • Now Broadcasting — the show on air now, with presenter and timeslot; updates automatically.
  • Listen Live — starts the radio; doubles as play/pause ("Playing Live").
  • Volume slider — adjusts the level.
  • News ticker — the station's top stories scrolling across.
  • On Air list — the day's full schedule; the live show is tagged ON NOW.

Talk to the Presenters

Where listeners send messages that presenters read out on air.

Controls:
  • Your name — the listener's name; it locks after the first message.
  • Type a message — the message to send.
  • Send — posts it to the chat staff see in the portal.
The number of people online is intentionally not shown to listeners.
