🎚 Put callers on air

You're already live in Miradio. Do this once, before any caller phones in:

  1. In Miradio, click TAB (top right, next to TALK).
  2. Choose the Chrome Tab section and pick this portal tab.
  3. Tick Also share tab audio, then click Share.
  4. Done. Every caller you take here now goes on air. Your voice stays on Miradio's MIC.

Keep this tab open for the whole show. Use Mute caller to take a caller off air. If you close or refresh this tab, share it again.

The stream runs 10 to 30 seconds behind real time. Ask callers to turn their radio off.
